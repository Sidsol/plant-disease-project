# Peach Tree Care Guide

## General Information
Peaches (*Prunus persica*) are stone fruits requiring moderate chill hours, full sun, and well-drained soil. They are more disease-prone than many fruit trees, making proper care and preventive sprays essential.

## Common Diseases
- **Bacterial Spot** (*Xanthomonas arboricola* pv. *pruni*): Shot-hole leaf spots, pitted fruit, twig cankers.
- **Brown Rot** (*Monilinia fructicola*): Fruit rot at maturity with tan fuzzy sporulation.
- **Peach Leaf Curl** (*Taphrina deformans*): Distorted, thickened, reddish leaves in spring.
- **Peach Scab** (*Cladosporium carpophilum*): Small dark spots on fruit.

## Growing Requirements
- **Sun**: Full sun.
- **Soil**: Well-drained, sandy loam preferred. pH 6.0-7.0.
- **Chill hours**: 400-1000 hours below 45°F depending on variety.
- **Spacing**: Standard 15-20 feet; dwarf 8-12 feet.
- **Pruning**: Open-center (vase) form for maximum air and light.

## Key Disease Prevention Tips
1. Apply copper at leaf fall and a dormant fungicide (chlorothalonil or lime-sulfur) for leaf curl before bud swell.
2. Choose bacterial spot-resistant varieties (Contender, PF series).
3. Thin fruit to prevent brown rot — crowded fruit clusters trap moisture.
4. Apply preventive fungicide sprays from shuck-split through harvest for brown rot.
5. Avoid overhead irrigation.
