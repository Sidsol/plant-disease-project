# Minor Crop Care Guides

## Blueberry (*Vaccinium* spp.)
- **Soil**: Acidic (pH 4.5-5.5), well-drained, high organic matter. Amend with peat moss or sulfur.
- **Common issues**: Mummy berry, Phytophthora root rot, stem blight, spotted wing drosophila.
- **Key tips**: Maintain acidic soil; avoid overhead watering during fruiting; prune old canes for vigor; use netting for bird protection.

## Raspberry (*Rubus* spp.)
- **Soil**: Well-drained, pH 5.8-6.5. Avoid wet sites (root rot risk).
- **Common issues**: Cane blight, spur blight, anthracnose, gray mold, raspberry bushy dwarf virus.
- **Key tips**: Remove spent floricanes after harvest (summer-bearing); prevent overcrowding of primocanes; avoid overhead watering; control weeds for air flow.

## Soybean (*Glycine max*)
- **Soil**: Well-drained, pH 6.0-7.0. Inoculate with *Bradyrhizobium* for nitrogen fixation.
- **Common issues**: Sudden death syndrome, white mold (Sclerotinia), soybean rust, soybean cyst nematode, frogeye leaf spot.
- **Key tips**: Select resistant varieties; rotate with non-host crops; scout regularly; manage row spacing for air flow; apply foliar fungicide when conditions warrant.

## General Guidance for Healthy Plants
For any crop identified as healthy by the classifier:
- Continue regular watering and fertilization practices.
- Monitor for early signs of disease weekly.
- Maintain proper plant spacing for air circulation.
- Remove any suspicious-looking leaves or fruit and investigate further.
- Follow recommended cultural practices for your specific crop and growing region.
