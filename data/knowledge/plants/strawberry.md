# Strawberry Care Guide

## General Information
Strawberries (*Fragaria × ananassa*) are popular small fruits grown as perennials (matted-row) or annuals (plasticulture). They are susceptible to several foliar and root diseases, and good management practices are essential for productivity.

## Common Diseases
- **Leaf Scorch** (*Diplocarpon earlianum*): Dark purple spots without white centers; scorched appearance.
- **Leaf Spot** (*Mycosphaerella fragariae*): Purple spots with white/gray centers.
- **Gray Mold / Botrytis** (*Botrytis cinerea*): Gray fuzzy mold on fruit, especially in wet weather.
- **Anthracnose** (*Colletotrichum* spp.): Sunken, dark fruit lesions; crown rot.
- **Verticillium Wilt**: Wilting, browning, and death of plants.

## Growing Requirements
- **Sun**: Full sun.
- **Soil**: Well-drained, sandy loam. pH 5.8-6.5. Avoid sites where solanaceous crops were recently grown (Verticillium risk).
- **Spacing**: 12-18 inches in rows 3-4 feet apart.
- **Watering**: 1-1.5 inches per week. Drip or trickle preferred. Avoid wetting fruit.

## Key Disease Prevention Tips
1. Renovate matted-row beds annually after harvest (mow, narrow, thin).
2. Use drip irrigation — avoid overhead watering, especially during fruiting.
3. Apply protective fungicides (captan) at bloom for leaf diseases and gray mold.
4. Remove and destroy infected fruit and foliage.
5. Plant resistant varieties when available and rotate planting sites.
